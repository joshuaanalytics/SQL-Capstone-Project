# One-Page Insights Summary

## SQL Data Analyst Capstone Project

### Overview

This project analyzes the Sakila sample database using SQL to identify customer spending patterns, revenue trends, product category performance, and purchasing behavior.

### Key Insights

**1. Top Customers**

The highest-spending customer was **KARL SEAL**, with total spending of **$221.55**. The analysis identified the top 10 customers based on their total payment amounts.

**2. Revenue Trend**

Monthly revenue increased substantially from **$4,823.44 in May 2005** to **$28,368.91 in July 2005**, which was the highest recorded monthly revenue. Revenue then decreased to **$24,070.14 in August 2005**.

**3. Month-over-Month Change**

The largest positive month-over-month increase occurred in **July 2005**, when revenue increased by **$18,739.02** compared with June. Revenue subsequently decreased in August.

**4. Category Performance**

**Sports** generated the highest revenue among the product categories, with **$5,314.21** in total revenue.

**5. Product Performance**

The analysis ranked the top three products by revenue within each category. This provides a more detailed view of which individual products contributed most strongly within each category.

**6. Customer Segmentation**

Customers were segmented into three spending tiers:

* **Low:** under $100
* **Medium:** $100–$149.99
* **High:** $150 or more

These thresholds were used to categorize customers based on their total spending.

**7. Customer Behavior**

The analysis identified **599 repeat buyers and 0 one-time buyers** among customers with payment records. Under the capstone definition, a repeat buyer is a customer with more than one recorded payment.

**8. Revenue Concentration**

The Sports category contributed **7.88% of total revenue**, making it the largest individual category contribution in the dataset.

### Business Takeaways

The analysis shows that revenue varied considerably across the available months and that Sports was the highest-revenue category. Customer spending and product-level rankings provide additional views that can be used for further business analysis.

### SQL Skills Demonstrated

This project applied SQL concepts including **JOINs, aggregation, GROUP BY, CASE statements, CTEs, LAG(), ROW_NUMBER(), window functions, ranking, and business-oriented data analysis**.
